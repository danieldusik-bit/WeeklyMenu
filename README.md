# Family Meal Planner

A mobile-friendly weekly meal planner and grocery shopping tracker — no app store needed, runs in any browser.

## Features

- **7-day menu plan** — breakfast, lunch and dinner for the whole week
- **Full recipes** — tap any meal to see ingredients, step-by-step method and a cooking tip
- **Shopping list** — all ingredients organised by category with quantities
- **Basket tracker** — tick items off as you shop, with a live progress bar
- **Category filter** — jump straight to meat, dairy, veg, etc. in the shop
- **Works on phone** — designed for one-handed use at the supermarket
- **Dark mode** — automatically follows your phone's setting
- **No install needed** — just open the link in any browser

## How to use

Open the app and use the two tabs at the top:

1. **Menu tab** — see the full week at a glance. Tap a day to expand it, then tap any meal to read the recipe.
2. **Shopping tab** — tap items to check them off as you put them in the basket. Use the category filters to jump between sections of the shop. Hit Reset to start fresh next week.
